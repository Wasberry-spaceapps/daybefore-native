# Naming & Domain: Day Before

## Reasoning
The name **Day Before** (daybefore.app) was chosen because it is backward-looking rather than forward-looking. It evokes the idea of a journal where you process yesterday while turning toward today. It is evocative rather than generic, and reads naturally in a sentence (e.g., "open your Day Before").

## Collision Check
A live lookup via Exa MCP revealed that the exact name "Day Before" is currently used by a 3D calendar utility app for Apple Vision Pro/iOS, but it is not an established journaling product or well-known brand. Another productivity system ("Ahead Challenge") references a "Day Before" framework, but there are no direct trademark conflicts with a major journaling platform. The name is clear to use. The domain `daybefore.app` is being purchased directly.
