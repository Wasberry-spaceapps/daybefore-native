# Design System & Creative Direction

## Rationale
This specific dark, spacious, and quietly-typeset treatment is designed for a space where someone writes about difficult emotions like jealousy or anger. It needs to feel safe, private, and calm, rather than clinical or gamified. By removing decorative chrome and using generous negative space, the interface steps out of the way, allowing the user's own words and reflections to hold focus without judgment.

## Color Tokens
- **Background**: Dark, near-black gradient (`#111111` to `#161616`). No decorative gradients on individual elements.
- **Text (Primary)**: Soft off-white (`#EAEAEA`) for maximum readability without harsh contrast.
- **Text (Secondary/Muted)**: Muted gray (`#888888`) for metadata, dates, and placeholders.
- **Accent**: A single soft accent color (`#7C98B3` or similar muted tone) reserved strictly for the text cursor or a single active-state indicator. Not used decoratively on borders or buttons.
- **Dividers**: Low-contrast, near-black thin lines (`#2A2A2A`) for separation.

## Typography
- **Primary Typeface**: A quiet, well-rounded sans-serif (e.g., Inter, system UI fonts, or similar plain sans). It must read as normal and readable first, and elegant second. No geometric novelty, stylistic flourishes, or unusual ligatures.
- **Hierarchy**: Differentiated by spacing and typographic weight, not by boxing elements in repeated card components.

## UI Elements
- **Containers**: No identical rounded-corner cards with matching soft shadows for sidebar sections. Sections are separated purely by typography and thin hairlines.
- **Lines & Dividers**: Simple, straight hairlines. No bends, curves, or skeuomorphic textures.
- **Chrome**: Minimal to none. Generous negative space used for grouping instead of explicit borders.
